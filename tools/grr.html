ndc — Number of Distinct Categories
How many non-overlapping groups your gage can reliably distinguish within process variation. Think of it as the resolution of your measurement system.
ndc = 1.41 × (PV ÷ Gage R&R)
1.41 = √2 — two distributions need ≥1 combined σ separation to be distinct
